Our Story

About Swaralaya

Swaralaya — meaning "the abode of musical notes" — is a classical arts academy nestled in Arani, Tiruvannamalai District, dedicated to preserving and passing on the rich heritage of South Indian performing arts.

Founded by Banu Manikandan, a distinguished Carnatic vocalist and veena exponent, the academy offers training in Carnatic Vocals, Bharatanatyam, Veena, and Keyboard — each taught by passionate and experienced gurus.

We believe that classical arts are not just a skill — they are a lifelong companion that shapes discipline, devotion, and cultural identity in every student.

Meet the Gurus

Our Faculty

BM

Banu Manikandan

Founder & Director

A gifted Carnatic vocalist and veena exponent, Banu Manikandan brings years of performance and teaching experience. She leads the vocal and veena programmes at Swaralaya with deep dedication to the classical tradition.

K

Keerthana

Bharatanatyam Instructor

Trained in the classical Bharatanatyam tradition, Keerthana imparts the nuances of adavus, abhinaya, and stage presentation with patience and artistry. She has guided numerous students through their arangetrams.

D

Dhatchayani

Bharatanatyam Instructor

Dhatchayani brings a graceful blend of technical precision and expressive storytelling to her Bharatanatyam classes. She works closely with students at every level, from basic footwork to full compositions.

Mo

Mosses

Keyboard Instructor

Mosses blends classical Carnatic theory with keyboard technique, helping students develop both musical understanding and practical playing skills on the keyboard.

NK

Nandha Kumar

Keyboard Instructor

Nandha Kumar guides keyboard students through scales, compositions, and classical exercises, making the learning process engaging and thorough for students of all levels.
